# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. This is a small shell plugin, not a venue for making
anyone feel unwelcome.

## What's expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings about context.
- Keep criticism on the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may say no to an idea. A `no`
  isn't a judgement of you.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience with zsh, with
  terminals, and with open source generally.

## What's not

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate derailing of issues.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space associated with the project, and to anyone taking part.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving badly, open an issue describing what happened, or use
GitHub's own [report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow if
you'd rather not do it in public.

Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity private where
possible, and may edit or delete comments, close issues, or block accounts.
